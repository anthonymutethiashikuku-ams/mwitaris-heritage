📖 Mwitari's Heritage Bylaws

1. Membership

  • All members must register with the group.
  • Members must comply with the group's rules.

2. Savings

  • Members shall make regular savings contributions.
  • Members should maintain their savings records.

3. Loans

  • Members must meet the loan eligibility requirements.
  • Loans must be repaid within the agreed period.

4. Meetings

  • Members should attend scheduled group meetings.
  • Important group decisions shall follow the agreed procedures.
